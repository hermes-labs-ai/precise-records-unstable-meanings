## Responsibility

The initial audit concept emerged during a Claude Code session and was selected, bounded, and developed into the present study by Rolando Bosch. Bosch determined the evidence boundary, adjudicated interpretations, controlled scope, made the final publication decisions, and retains responsibility for the work.

No AI system is listed as an accountable author. Responsibility for errors, release decisions, corrections, and public claims remains human.

## Hermes Autonomous Lab contribution

The Hermes Autonomous Lab—operating through Claude Code and OpenAI Codex sessions—performed substantial work in:

- corpus inventory and source mapping;
- analysis and validation programming;
- aggregate extraction and visualization;
- privacy and consistency checks;
- adversarial criticism;
- method specification;
- publication architecture; and
- prose drafting and revision.

The work was executed under human-defined constraints, evidence boundaries, stop conditions, and publication gates. Human interventions changed scope, interpretation, and final positioning.

## Review disclosure

A fresh-context model attempted to falsify the analysis during development. Its accepted findings included a privacy leak in shareable artifacts, gap-dominated duration tails, and an invalid rate denominator. This was an internal challenge mechanism, not independent peer review or scientific validation.
